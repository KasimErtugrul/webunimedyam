## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a focused, high-contrast digital broadcast aesthetic engineered specifically for Turkish higher-education media and campus life streaming. The interface targets modern university students, prospective academics, and alumni who expect an experience on par with high-end global video streaming platforms, paired with the academic authority of university credentials.

The emotional tone balances intellectual prestige with late-night campus energy: focused, electric, authoritative, and frictionless. 

Drawing from modern **Dark Glassmorphism** and **High-Contrast Digital Editorial** aesthetics, the visual structure relies on pitch obsidian voids, deep slate structural layers, and razor-sharp emerald energy accents. Vibrant university heraldry and campus video thumbnails anchor the visual landscape, while neon-tinged emerald guides navigation, verification status, and playback state without distracting from user-generated and campus broadcast video content.

## Colors
The color palette creates a layered dark environment optimized for OLED mobile displays and long-form video consumption, prioritizing deep visual hierarchy and high readability.

### Canvas & Structural Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#0B0F17`):** Deep obsidian void used for global app backgrounds, video player surrounds, and edge-bleed containers.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#131B2A`):** Dark slate tier used for bottom navigation bars, fixed headers, and search docks.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1B2436`):** Card surface and interactive tier used for video list items, filter rails, campus profile headers, and modal sheets.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#232F46`):** Elevated utility tier for dropdown menus, active segment buttons, and floating pill chips.
- **Structural Border (`#2A364F`):** Subtle structural divider stroke defining edges without drawing focus away from dynamic media.

### Accent & Feedback Palette
- **Primary Emerald (`#10B981`):** The primary signature brand hue. Applied to active tab icons, primary calls-to-action, playback progress bars, and university verified checkmarks.
- **Emerald Deep (`#059669`):** Pressed/hover state for emerald interactive triggers and active chip fills.
- **Neon Emerald Glow (`#34D399`):** Accent highlights, focus rings, tag pill borders, and active status indicators.
- **Live Crimson Pulse (`#EF4444`):** Exclusive broadcast state indicator denoting real-time university symposiums, live campus concerts, and active lectures.
- **Text & Foreground:** 
  - Primary text: `#F8FAFC` (100% white-slate for headlines and titles)
  - Secondary text: `#94A3B8` (Slate-light for creator names, timestamps, and metadata)
  - Tertiary / Muted: `#64748B` (Inactive icons, placeholders, and secondary metrics)

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans is utilized across all typographic roles. Its clean geometric curves provide instant legibility on dense mobile feed cards while maintaining high-impact authority for university identities and live badges.

- **Headlines:** Set with tightened letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`) and bold-to-extrabold weights to command visual priority in crowded video lists.
- **Body:** Neutral tracking and standard weight (`400`) ensure university course descriptions and video summaries remain strain-free against dark card layers.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Elevated weights (`600` and `700`) paired with uppercase handling for micro-tags (e.g., `CANLI`, `4K`, `KAMPÜS`, `RESMİ`) ensure immediate scannability at 10px and 12px.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a strict 4-column fluid mobile grid expanding into a 6-to-8-column structure on tablet form factors.

- **Mobile Viewport Structure:** 
  - Standard edge canvas margin: `1rem` (16px).
  - Feed card gutter: `1rem` (16px) vertical stack; horizontal preview carousels use `0.75rem` (12px) gutter with trailing peek offset (`24px`).
  - Safe-area bottom padding accounts for the 64px docked navigation bar + device navigation pill inset.
- **Rhythm & Touch Insets:**
  - Interactive touch targets maintain a minimum vertical clearance of `48px`.
  - Icon-only actions use `space-sm` internal padding within a minimum `40px` touch bounding frame.
  - Video card meta stacks use `space-xs` (4px) between badges and title, and `space-sm` (8px) between thumbnail and channel meta blocks.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through tonal layering and selective luminous green glow rather than heavy dropshadows, avoiding muddy contrasts on OLED displays.

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Canvas `#0B0F17` — Flat non-interactive backdrop.
- **Card Layer (Elevation 1):** Background `#1B2436` with a crisp `1px` stroke in `#2A364F`. Dropshadows are withheld; structural clarity comes from border contrast.
- **Hover / Active Card (Elevation 2):** Background shifts to `#232F46` with a focused stroke tint of `#34D399` at 30% opacity.
- **Floating Controls & Bottom Navigation (Elevation 3):** Glassmorphic surface using `#131B2A` at 85% opacity with a `20px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), anchored by a top hairline border of `#2A364F` (50% opacity).
- **Emerald Glow Effects:**
  - Active video progress handles and live stream badges utilize a soft ambient glow: `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`.
  - Live indicator dot incorporates a pulsed crimson glow: `0 0 10px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.60)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy adopts standard **Rounded** metrics (`0.5rem` / 8px base) to strike a balance between high-tech streaming interfaces and institutional dependability.

- **Base Containers & Video Thumbnails:** `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px) corner radius, softening dense grid lists while maintaining edge definition.
- **Interactive Buttons & Form Elements:** `0.5rem` (8px) radius for precise, modern geometric feel.
- **Badges, Filter Chips & Avatar Verification Markers:** Fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) to immediately signal category filtering and status information distinct from square media bounding boxes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled `#10B981` background, `#0B0F17` bold typography, `0.5rem` border radius. On active press: `#059669` with scale transform (`0.98`).
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `1px` border in `#2A364F`, `#F8FAFC` text. On active press: background `#1B2436`.
- **Icon Buttons:** `#1B2436` container, `1px` border in `#2A364F`, centered 20px icon in `#94A3B8`. Hover/active shifts icon to `#34D399`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Inactive:** `#131B2A` background, `1px` border in `#2A364F`, text `#94A3B8`, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `space-xs` vertical and `space-md` horizontal padding.
- **Active:** Deep emerald fill `#059669` at 20% opacity, crisp `#10B981` border, text `#34D399`, font weight `600`.

### Cards (Media & University)
- **Video Stream Card:** 16:9 aspect ratio media container with `0.75rem` radius. Bottom right duration chip (`#0B0F17` at 85% opacity, `label-sm`). Below media: avatar (`36px` circle), title (`headline-sm`, max 2 lines), university name accompanied by a verified checkmark badge (`#10B981`), and metadata line (`body-sm` in `#94A3B8`).
- **University Profile Card:** Banner cover, overlapping circular crest with an emerald verified ring, metric counters (Öğrenci Sayısı, Yayınlar, Takipçi), and an outline "Takip Et" button.

### Badges & Verification Indicators
- **CANLI (Live) Badge:** Crimson background (`#EF4444`), white `label-sm` text, prepended by a 6px white pulsating circle with ambient red glow.
- **University Verified Badge:** 14px emerald disc (`#10B981`) featuring a sharp white checkmark glyph, permanently anchored to institutional names.
- **Quality / Category Badges:** Semi-transparent dark slate backdrop (`#131B2A` at 80% opacity) with `#94A3B8` `label-sm` text.

### Inputs & Search Bar
- **Search Field:** `#1B2436` base, `1px` border `#2A364F`, leading search icon in `#64748B`. Placeholder in `#64748B`. On focus: border transitions to `#10B981` with subtle `0 0 0 2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` ring.

### Lists & Segmented Controls
- **Lists:** Borderless flat rows with `#2A364F` divider line spanning content width (offset by avatar margin).
- **Segmented Tabs:** Horizontal full-width bar on `#131B2A`. Inactive item: `#94A3B8`. Active item: `#F8FAFC` with a bottom `2px` sliding indicator in `#10B981`.

### Bottom Navigation Bar (5-Tab Dock)
- **Bar Surface:** Fixed bottom, height `64px`, glassmorphic `#131B2A` (85% opacity + `20px` blur), border-t `1px` `#2A364F`.
- **Items:** 
  1. *Ana Sayfa* (Home icon)
  2. *Keşfet* (Compass icon)
  3. *Üniversiteler* (Academic cap / columns icon)
  4. *Ara* (Magnifying glass icon)
  5. *Profil* (User avatar circle)
- **Interaction State:** Inactive icons and text in `#64748B`; active icon and label switch to `#10B981` with an emerald micro-dot indicator below the label.
